# Petal Technologies — sample brand guide

## Brand architecture
- Company: **Petal Technologies**
- Product: **Petal AI**
- Tagline: **Knowledge. Intelligence. Connection.**
- Positioning: source-aware knowledge assistance and human support workflows for organizations and professional communities.

This is a proposed sample identity for this software project. It is not a statement that Petal Technologies is legally registered.

## Color system
- Deep navy: `#17324D` — primary text, headings, and strong navigation actions.
- Botanical green: `#2E8B57` — primary brand accent and active states.
- Soft mint: `#7FD3A3` — supporting highlights and gentle surfaces.
- Warm amber: `#E0A030` — human review and escalation status only.
- White: `#FFFFFF` — main application surface.
- Pale green surface: `#E8F5EE` — subtle selected and highlighted states.

Use generous white space, clear hierarchy, restrained rounded surfaces, and accessible contrast. Avoid heavy gradients, dark full-screen backgrounds, and excessive animation.

## Logo direction
A simple four-petal mark with a small navy center. Keep the mark clear at small sizes. Do not use ISACA's protected logo or visual marks as the Petal company logo.

## Product voice
Clear, calm, transparent, and helpful. Petal AI should distinguish source-backed answers from uncertainty and route unresolved or conflicting questions to a person.

## ISACA context
This application supports workflows involving ISACA chapter resources. It is an independent software demonstration and must not imply official ISACA ownership, endorsement, or affiliation. Use ISACA names and assets only where authorized.
